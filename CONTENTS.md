## 9. Research integrity and scope

- A **perspective-dependent observation** is not automatically a causal intervention.
- A **score difference** is not necessarily a decision, trajectory, or learning difference.
- **Behavioral equivalence** is conditional on an explicit comparison contract, evidence, and a time horizon.
- **Component compatibility** does not guarantee system-level contract preservation.
- **Biological inspiration** is not proof of shared neural mechanisms.
- **Structural transparency** does not imply full interpretability of an embedded LLM.
- **Open evolution** does not mean unrestricted self-modification.
- **Negative results and counterexamples** are legitimate research outputs when their conditions are recorded.
