## Overview

This inaugural release develops **Beyond UIERU: Dynamic Structural Intelligence and Open Research Relay (BUI-DSI)**, an open research agenda for studying intelligence units as context-sensitive, perspective-dependent, observable, composable, and evolving systems.

Building on the Universal Intelligence Evolution and Relay Unit (UIERU) concept and earlier Structural Intelligence (SI) research, BUI-DSI investigates how dynamic intelligence can be **observed, compared, queried, substituted, composed, governed, and evolved** without collapsing distinct kinds of evidence or claiming that proposed mathematical properties have already been established.

A second contribution is **Open Research Relay**: portable research pieces that expose unresolved problems, candidate models, hypotheses, evidence, counter-evidence, validation plans, and interfaces for independent scientific work.

## Key research directions

**Perspective-aware observation and comparison.** BUI-DSI distinguishes an observational view from an intervention, a score from a decision, a decision from a trajectory, and a trajectory from evidence of learning.

**Conditional dynamic equivalence.** Behavioral equivalence and substitution are defined relative to explicit perspective, context, history, time horizon, tolerance, evidence, and contract assumptions. Local equivalence does not automatically guarantee system-level preservation.

**Governed structural evolution.** Candidate changes may arise from learning, replacement, composition, and recursive self-improvement (RSI). Evaluation, counter-evidence, contract checks, and rollback governance remain necessary; open evolution does not imply unrestricted self-modification.

**RSI–UIERU convergence.** The release introduces an exploratory, bidirectional research direction: LLM-based RSI may supply candidate improvements, while UIERU-style interfaces may improve structural observation, comparison, and governance. This is a hypothesis and integration opportunity, **not** an assertion that RSI and UIERU are equivalent.

**Biological inspiration with scientific boundaries.** Dynamic organizational analogies may help formulate experiments; they do not establish that biological brains literally implement UIERU or Scoring Forest architectures.

**Open Research Relay.** Problems, assumptions, proofs, counterexamples, negative results, and validation procedures are first-class research artifacts intended for independent extension and critique.

## Scientific status and limitations

This release is a **research proposal and documentation collection**. In particular:

- Candidate algebraic properties, bisimulation claims, and composition rules require formal proof or counterexamples.
- Proposed hypotheses require preregistered or otherwise reproducible empirical tests and suitable baselines.
- Illustrative SQL, YAML, and conceptual schemas do not establish production readiness.
- Structural traceability does not, by itself, explain the internal reasoning of an opaque LLM.
- No benchmark superiority, biological mechanism, or deployment-safety claim is established by this release.

Contributions that disprove or narrow a proposed claim are welcome and should retain provenance.
