Beyond UIERU: Dynamic Structural Intelligence and Open Research Relay (BUI-DSI), by Sizhe Tan and GPT-Obot, presents an open research framework for studying intelligence units as dynamic, context-dependent systems that observe, evaluate, act, learn, compose, and evolve. Building on the Universal Intelligence Evolution and Relay Unit (UIERU) perspective, the project investigates Perspective Masking, Perspective Differential Trees, Perspective-Aware Structural Query, conditional dynamic behavioral equivalence, Relay composition and contracts, and open structural evolution. It also explores research interfaces with recursive self-improvement (RSI), LLM-based agents, formal methods, and computational neuroscience. The repository includes eight core papers, three illustrative cases, five open Research Relay topics, four interface specifications, and a validation roadmap. Its central aim is to make research questions, candidate models, evidence, counter-evidence, and validation methods portable across disciplines. Proposed mathematical properties and biological analogies are presented as hypotheses or open questions, not established results.
